About Me
// logs · entry 001

Hey — I'm pafuu, an upcoming game developer with one big vision: every game I make exists in the same shared universe. Different genres, different stories — but one world underneath it all. Think Azeroth, but mine. Built in Unity.

On the side I'm genuinely fascinated by the practical side of cybersecurity — how everyday users stay safe online. Password managers like Bitwarden, good OPSEC habits, understanding how your traffic actually moves through a network. The boring stuff that actually matters.

I have a habit of finding a new hobby, diving in headfirst, and refusing to come back up. Oh, and I'm all in on gaming — always have been, always will be.

FAQ
// frequently asked questions
Why one shared universe across all your games?
Every game I make is set in the same world — different genres, different stories, but one universe underneath. Like how Azeroth ties together WoW, Warcraft, and Hearthstone. I want to build something with that kind of depth, but entirely mine.
What engine do you use?
Unity. It hits the sweet spot between power and flexibility for the kinds of games I want to make. I'm always tinkering with it and learning new corners of the engine.
What cybersecurity areas interest you?
Mostly the practical, everyday side — how regular users can actually stay safe. Things like using Bitwarden for password management, understanding your home network, knowing what data you're exposing. Networking fundamentals are also a big one for me.
Are you open to collaboration?
Absolutely. Whether it's a game project, a security discussion, or just someone who wants to geek out about the same things — hit me up via the contact page.
What are you currently playing?
Something. Always something. Gaming is a core part of who I am — it's what made me want to make games in the first place. Ask me and I'll probably give you a very enthusiastic answer.
